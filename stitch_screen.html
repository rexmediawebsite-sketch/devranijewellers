Inquiries & Clarity

Frequently Addressed Questions

Clear, transparent answers regarding our certifications, valuation, and custom process.

How do I verify the BIS Hallmark on my jewellery? expand_more

Every piece from Aurelia is micro-laser inscribed with three official marks: the BIS logo, the purity grade (e.g. 22K916 or 24K999), and a unique 6-character alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification). You can enter this HUID directly into the official Government 'BIS CARE' mobile app to verify artisan origin and purity certification in real-time.

How does the WhatsApp consultation and purchase process work? expand_more

Our WhatsApp concierge connects you directly with a senior jewellery specialist. We share high-resolution 4K macro video clips, model draping perspectives, and complete weight-breakdown invoices. Once finalized, payments are processed via encrypted banking gateways, and your piece is dispatched via insured, tamper-evident courier.

Can I bring my ancestral gold to remodel into a modern design? expand_more

Yes. Our Remodelling Atelier conducts non-destructive XRF assay testing in your presence at our boutiques to establish purity. The full bullion value is credited 100% towards your new commission with zero deduction on gold content.

What is your lifetime exchange and valuation policy? expand_more

We provide a transparent lifetime buyback and exchange guarantee: 100% of current gold market rate calculated against net gold weight, and 90% of prevailing diamond value for natural diamonds. Each transaction is accompanied by a formal credit certificate.

Are diamond certificates provided with solitaires? expand_more

Every solitaire above 0.30ct includes an original, verifiable certificate from either the GIA (Gemological Institute of America) or IGI (International Gemological Institute), matching the microscopic laser inscription on the diamond's girdle.
